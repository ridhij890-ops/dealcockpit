CO-FOUNDERS & CO-ARCHITECTS

Ridhi Jain & Ananya Agrawal

Final-year BBA Finance students at NMIMS

Ridhi Jain and Ananya Agrawal are the co-founders and co-architects of DealCockpit. As final-year BBA Finance students at NMIMS, they brought their shared interest in venture finance, risk thinking, and product design into an interactive analytics concept.

Together, they shaped the modeling approach, risk engine, stress tests, and product strategy. Their goal is to help teams ask better questions about a deal—not to replace judgment, diligence, or the people in the room.

THE DESIGN PRINCIPLE

More transparency.
More human judgment.

DealCockpit makes its demo assumptions visible so investors can inspect inputs, test a scenario, and discuss what matters next.

01

Show the assumptions

Keep the inputs and simple coefficient contributions in view.

02

Invite a what-if

Make it easy to explore how the result moves when an assumption changes.

03

Keep humans in the loop

Use the interface to support careful discussion—not as an investment verdict.
